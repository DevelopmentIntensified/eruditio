# Separate gleaning from memorization review

A reading session's primary output is a **gleaning**: the reader's own takeaway paired with source context, plus curated interpretation when the content provides it. Review and Mastery remain reserved for deliberately curated, memorizable material; arbitrary reading segments must not become review items or the primary learning loop.

## Consequences

- The normal completion flow can create a source-linked gleaning without grading recall; the reader may explicitly discard the reflection.
- Review infrastructure may remain dormant until eligible memorizable content exists.
- Future review surfaces must state what is being memorized and why.
