# Eruditio — store listing (draft, ticket 11)

## Title
Eruditio — read instead of doomscrolling

## Short description (80 chars max)
A quiet pause when you open your scroll apps — and real reading in its place.

## Full description
Your hand reaches for your phone before you decide anything. Eruditio meets you
at that moment: a brief pause, a breath, and a choice. Go straight to the app —
it is always your call — or read a page of a classic instead.

- **Friction, not blocks.** A 10-second pause with a breathing moment stands
  between you and your most-opened scroll apps. You choose: continue, or read.
  Every interception is recorded honestly — continued or replaced.
- **A real library, offline.** 30 public-domain classics bundled in the app:
  Plato, Marcus Aurelius, Epictetus, KJV selections, Spurgeon sermons,
  Augustine, missionary biographies. No network, no feeds, no adjacency.
- **Reading that pays back.** Completing a session creates review cards for
  what you read. Spaced-repetition reviews move concepts from familiar to
  proficient to mastered — and decay if you neglect them. Mastery is honest.
- **No accounts, no sync, no trackers.** Everything lives on your device.
  Zero analytics SDKs, zero ads. Your habits are nobody's data.
- **Pay once.** One purchase owns the app. Future content tracks arrive as
  optional packs. No subscription, ever.

Claims discipline: we claim only what the evidence supports — friction reduces
openings; review-based practice improves retention. We claim nothing about
cognition training or clinical outcomes.
