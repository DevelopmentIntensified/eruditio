# 23: Gleaning output

**What to build:** A meaningful reading completion leaves a source-linked gleaning: the reader writes one takeaway in their own words, then compares it with curated context and the original passage. Gleanings are collected for later browsing; they are not graded and do not enter the spaced-repetition ledger.

**Blocked by:** Reader flow and content/source identity (03, 05, 06).
